Minecraft in the browser. Pick a client or a version, then click the game once to grab mouse control.

Game 05

Heads up: the 1.16.5 builds are the heaviest. They need a decent device with plenty of memory — on an older laptop or phone they can stutter or fail to load. Try 1.5.2 or 1.8.8 instead.

Loading Astra Client 1.8.8
  • Click inside the game once so it can lock your mouse. Esc releases it.
  • Client builds are single files of 18-56 MB, so the first load takes a moment; after that the browser has them cached.
  • A black screen in 26.2 is its loading screen, not a crash — let it finish loading. For other builds, give a blank frame a moment before using Reload game.
