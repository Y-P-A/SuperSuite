Bars — 2

Tap squares in the note grid to write the tune — the top row is the highest note. The drum strip along the bottom is the beat. Press play and it loops.

Sound
Drum
  • Tap to paint. Click a square to add a note, drag across to add a run of them, or drag over notes again to rub them out.
  • Pick your sound. Choose the note instrument and the drum underneath — every square you tap uses the sound you have picked.
  • Space plays and stops. Your songs save in this browser, and Export file keeps a copy you can share.
